Case study Head of Growth, 2025 to 2026

The habit was already in the data.

Isha Foundation's meditation app. The work: a new user's first three days.

Activation
69%→ to 80%
+11 pts vs the previous 90 days. ≈135K users.
90-day churn
−17%
New users lost within 90 days.
DAU/MAU
+11%
≈20% to 22.2%.

How it happened

Problem

Activation sat at 69%. Nobody knew which early habits made people stay.

  1. 01

    Read the data.

    • 24 hours A first meditation within a day of signing up meant much higher 60 and 90-day retention.
    • 3 in a row Three meditations in a row meant far less churn and more invites.

  2. 02

    Talk to users.

    The people who stayed had accountability and healthy competition with friends and family. The data said where. Users said why.

  3. 03

    Change the first 72 hours.

    24h 3 in a row

    1. First open

      01 First meditation moved into registration. Nothing else on first open.

      Aimed at the 24-hour window.

    2. After session one

      02 Commit to a 7, 14 or 21-day streak, a daily time and reminders.

      Aimed at three in a row.

    3. Day three onward

      03 The streak became a shareable stats card. Bragging rights, and a flywheel.

      Healthy competition. Sharing +21%.

    4. Every open

      04 Contacts who meditated today.

      Accountability, as social proof.

    Around these: challenges, leaderboards, and thousands of user testimonials feeding product decisions.

  4. 04

    Measure.

    Beyond the three up top:

    Sessions per user
    3.2 → to 3.6
    Conservative 180-day aggregate.
    In-app sharing
    +21%
    Shareable streak cards, A/B tested.

I made the calls and set the direction. The execution was a collaborative effort of an amazing product team.
