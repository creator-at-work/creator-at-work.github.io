The training

Between Isha Foundation's marketing team and starting my own agency, I spent July to November 2023 in a residential Classical Hatha Yoga teacher training at Isha Foundation. 1,750 hours. I am a trained Classical Hatha Yoga teacher.

iResetLife

In December 2024 I co-founded iResetLife, a mental-wellness venture that brings Classical Hatha Yoga together with behavioural psychology and modern wellbeing frameworks. We built it across India and Africa: product development, market creation, and commercial and nonprofit work.

I led a wellness initiative with young women in Maasai Mara, Kenya.

Why it is not a side note

I came to yoga and philosophy looking for my own answers, not prescribed ones. It turned out to be the same question I work on in products: what makes a person come back to something once the novelty is gone.
