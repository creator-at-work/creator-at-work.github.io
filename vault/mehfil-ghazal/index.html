About

What is Mehfil?

A quiet listening room for original ghazals. Every recording here is sung by its original vocalist, with no modern remakes, covers, lofi edits, or multi-hour jukeboxes.

Five moods

Browse or start radio by Bireh, Naraazgi, Izhaar, Tanhai, or Maikada.

No repeats in a day

Radio remembers what you heard over the last 24 hours, even if you pause, stop, or return later.

Live lyrics & translation

Click anywhere on the bottom player bar to open live Roman Urdu lyrics with English meaning underneath.

Listen together

Share a live room link so a friend or partner hears the same ghazal at the same moment.

Sleep timer

Set a gentle fade-out timer or stop quietly at the end of the current ghazal.
