Case study N° 01 Miracle of Mind, Isha Foundation Head of Growth, 2025 to 2026 The habit was already in the data. ProblemActivation sat at 69%, with no clear picture of which early behaviours made people stay. ApproachRead the data for patterns, then talk to users one on one. What changedFour changes to a new user's first 72 hours: first meditation inside registration, a streak commitment with a daily time, shareable streak cards, and friends who meditated today. Result 69% → to 80% activation −17% 90-day churn +11% DAU/MAU 3.2 → to 3.6 sessions per user +21% in-app sharing Read the case →

Notebook

Entries.

  1. N° 02Miracle of Mind

    Streak cards people wanted to share

    Problem
    The users who stayed had healthy competition with friends and family. The product gave them no way to show it.
    What changed
    Streak cards became shareable. A/B tests ran inside product launches.
    Result
    +21% in-app sharing

    The "healthy competition" finding came from one-on-one calls with users.

  2. N° 03Miracle of Mind

    Meditation inside ChatGPT

    Problem
    Guided meditation only reached people who opened the app.
    What changed
    I led development of a guided meditation experience inside ChatGPT. A new interface, and a new distribution channel.
    Result
    Shipped

    Result
    Shipped

  5. N° 06Isha Foundation

    Insight Leadership Program

    Problem
    More registrations, without paying more for each one.
    Approach
    A mix of CBO and ABO campaign structures.
    Result
    2× registrations, −60% CPA

    CBO: budget set at campaign level. ABO: budget set per ad set. CPA: cost per acquisition.

  6. N° 07Isha Foundation

    Save Soil

    Problem

    What changed
    Performance marketing, optimised for cost per view.
    Result
    −25% CPV, 1B+ impressions

    CPV: cost per view.
