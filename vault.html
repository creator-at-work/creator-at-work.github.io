Jatin Bhatt / Weekend Vault

Weekend Vault.

A collection of cool (or not) stuff I built over the weekends with Claude, Codex and more recently Neon just for the fun of it.

Signal adds the counts, boundaries and what each build runs on.

  1. V° 01 Listening Room

    Mehfil

    A ghazal-only music player made for those of us still living in the 60s era in our musical taste. Pick a mood like Bireh, Naraazgi or Izhaar, set a sleep timer, start a 24-hour no-repeat radio, or share a live link to listen together in sync.

    Original recordings 5 moods Live synced rooms

    No covers, remakes, lofi edits or multi-hour jukeboxes. Synced listen-together rooms run on Cloudflare Durable Objects over Server-Sent Events, and your 24-hour listening ledger stays in your browser so stopping and restarting radio never repeats a ghazal.

    Open →
  2. V° 02 Product Craft

    The Product YouTube

    A YouTube made only for product videos, sortable by likes, recency, views and least dislikes. Give it a topic and how much time you have, and it builds a marathon playlist across creators without repetition, overlap or slop.

    252 vetted talks 8 topic boundaries 61 hard-block filters

    Static catalogue exported from verified product channels. Every video passes topical boundary rules to filter out career hype and generic tech noise.

    Open →
  3. V° 03 Philosophy

    The Humbler

    A place you go to get humbled. Type your original genius idea on life, philosophy, spirituality or human nature, and it maps it back to the oldest person and text that said it thousands of years before you.

    81 ancient origins 4,600+ years spanned 6 classical scripts

    Client-side matching across 81 canonical entries in Sanskrit, Greek, Chinese, Hebrew, Pali and Latin, with an optional live scholar lookup.

    Open →
  4. V° 04 Cartography & History

    Atlas of Lives

    A clutter-free visual storyboard of 15 of history's most influential people, arranged chronologically from Aristotle to Alan Turing. Each life unfolds at a glance on a single map as numbered points from birth to death, with only the dates, ages, places and details that matter.

    15 lives 50m world atlas 0 external APIs

    Built with D3 v7 and an embedded Natural Earth 50m TopoJSON globe. Zooming out reveals contemporaneous empires and historic cities for each era.

    Open →
  5. V° 05 Cinema & Series

    Afterdark

    The highest-effort part of the weekend for me is finding the right movie to watch before opening the box of pizza. So I built a simple tool that suggests your next watch and where to stream it based on mood, genre and IMDb rating.

    1.3M IMDb titles 8 moods India streaming lookup

    Ranks 1,301,970 IMDb film and series records exhaustively inside a browser Web Worker backed by IndexedDB, paired with a Cloudflare Worker for cast search and India streaming availability. A passkey-gated personal edition on the Worker keeps a private watch journal and learned taste model.

    Open →
  6. V° 06 Listening Sanctuary

    Pahaad

    A quiet Kumauni-first music sanctuary for original voices of the hills — from Gopal Babu Goswami, Heera Singh Rana and Mohan Upreti to Pappu Karki and curated Garhwali & Jaunsari greats. Tune an analog brass dial across six Himalayan moods like Niswaas, Mait and Chaumaas, with live Roman lyrics and poetic English translations.

    95 original geet 6 Pahadi moods Roman lyrics & English

    Original archival voices only with zero DJ remixes or jukeboxes. Pairs real 2400px Kumaon & Uttarakhand photography with a 24-hour no-repeat browser ledger, 29 full-lyric classics, 44 clickable Pahadi glossary terms, Baajyu's Notes, Sleep Timer, and synthesized mountain soundscapes.

    Open →

Each build opens in its own atmosphere and runs entirely in your browser with no sign-up and no tracking.
